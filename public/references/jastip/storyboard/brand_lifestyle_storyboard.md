# KeepTrip — "Don't Miss It" Brand Story

**Format:** Instagram Reel / TikTok · **Duration:** ~42s · **Shots:** 8 (4–7s each) · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 (1080×1920)
**Style:** Cinematic live-action · **Language:** English captions · **Structure:** Pattern C — Atmosphere → Emotional Climax → Brand reveal
**CTA:** Download KeepTrip · **App name:** KeepTrip

> **Creative premise:** Every iconic travel memory happened because someone was fully present in that moment. This video never shows app features. It shows the *space* KeepTrip creates — the difference between being *in* the trip and being *distracted by* the trip.

> **What makes this different from the feature videos:** No UI. No mockups. No motion graphic. Pure cinematic storytelling. KeepTrip appears once — in the last 10 seconds — as the punchline, not the pitch.

---

## Visual Theme (lock across every shot)

| Layer | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Warm golden amber (echoing `#DB9F5C`) for sunlit scenes · deep teal blue-black for night scenes · neon coral and cyan accents from Tokyo practical lighting · clean bone white for interior/transition shots |
| **Film look** | Anamorphic 2.39:1 reframed to 9:16 crop · subtle warm grade (highlights push golden, shadows pull teal) · 35mm grain overlay at 3% · gentle lens flares on backlit shots · NOT clean digital |
| **Lighting** | Day: golden-hour backlighting, subjects rim-lit by the sun. Night: practical neon and warm incandescent from the city. No artificial-looking studio light anywhere. |
| **Lens character** | 35mm equivalent · shallow DOF on close-ups (faces, hands, food) · wide on establishing and group shots · slight vignette at edges |
| **Motion language** | Gimbal-smooth for establishing shots · gentle handheld sway for intimate moments · slow push-in or rack focus on emotional beats · **no fast cuts** — this video breathes |
| **Characters** | Consistent trio across all shots: (1) **Ayu** — Indonesian woman, late 20s, short dark hair, cream linen shirt; (2) **Budi** — Indonesian man, mid 20s, navy blue hoodie; (3) **Rini** — Indonesian woman, early 30s, wire-frame round glasses, olive cardigan. |
| **Setting** | Tokyo — Senso-ji temple, Tsukiji outer market, Shibuya crossing, rain-slicked neon street at night, departure hall at Haneda Airport. |

---

## Narrative Arc

```
Anticipation → Arrival → The moment they almost missed → Full presence → Joy → Bittersweet ending → Brand reveal
```

The emotional engine: every shot builds toward the question *"Are you actually here right now, or are you somewhere else?"* The brand answer is: **KeepTrip keeps the elsewhere — so you can be here.**

### Shot 1 (0–5s · 5s) — Hook / Anticipation
**Purpose:** Stop the scroll instantly with something visually arresting and emotionally charged.
**Composition:** ECU on an airplane window. Outside: a sea of clouds bathed in golden pre-dawn light. A hand — Ayu's — presses flat against the cold glass, fingers spread.
**Camera move:** Locked off, micro-push-in. The hand slowly relaxes as if releasing something.
**Lighting:** Natural golden hour from outside the window, warm rim on the hand against the blue-tinted cabin interior.
**Subject:** Ayu's hand on the airplane window. No face visible — just the hand and the light.
**Action:** Hand presses against glass, pauses, then the fingers slowly uncurl — letting go of whatever is behind her.

**Prompt to copy:**

**Caption (on-screen):** *(none — let the image breathe)*
**Audio direction:** Cabin ambient hum fading out; then a single breath in; music begins — soft fingerpicked acoustic guitar, unhurried. The sound of the trip starting.

**Lighting:** Warm incandescent market lighting — amber and golden. Practical lanterns in background bokeh.
**Subject:** Tsukiji outer market, early morning. The food handoff and Rini's reaction.
**Action:** Vendor hands over tamagoyaki → Rini takes a bite → eyes close → small laugh. She says something to Budi off-frame without looking.

**Prompt to copy:**

### Shot 4 (16–20s · 4s) — Conflict / The moment almost lost
**Purpose:** The brief tension beat. Quick. We don't dwell here — just enough to feel the friction.
**Composition:** MS — a restaurant table. The meal is over. Three empty ramen bowls. Budi is staring at his phone, brow furrowed, calculator app visible. Ayu and Rini wait, visibly wanting to leave. The magic of the moment is draining.
**Camera move:** Slow push-in toward Budi's furrowed face, phone glow reflecting on his glasses. Slight underexposure — this shot feels heavier.
**Lighting:** Harsh restaurant overhead fluorescent — intentionally less warm than other shots. This is the only "bad light" shot.
**Subject:** Post-meal check-splitting tension. Budi doing math on his phone.
**Action:** Budi taps the calculator, frowns, taps again. Ayu checks the time. Rini looks out the window. Nobody is talking. The trip has paused.

**Prompt to copy:**
> Cinematic live-action, vertical 9:16, medium shot inside a Tokyo ramen restaurant after a meal, three empty bowls on the table, a young Southeast Asian man in a navy hoodie staring at his phone calculator with a furrowed brow, phone screen glow reflecting on his face, two women at the table waiting — one checks her watch, one looks away at the window, harsh cool overhead fluorescent lighting intentionally flat and draining compared to warm shots, slow push-in toward the man's face, 35mm grain, 4s, 9:16, cinematic 1080p, synchronized audio

**Caption (on-screen, holds full 4s):** *"Don't let this be the memory."*

## Post-Production Checklist

- [ ] Generate Shots 1–7 as AI live-action video (Kling, Runway, Veo, Sora — all work with these prompts).
- [ ] Generate Shot 8 separately as a motion graphic (After Effects, Jitter, or CapCut title card).
- [ ] Stitch in DaVinci Resolve / Premiere / CapCut.
- [ ] Apply one consistent **warm anamorphic LUT** across Shots 1–7 (DaVinci's "Kodak 5219" emulation or similar). Shot 4 intentionally gets a **flatter, cooler grade** — pull the warmth out by 40% for that beat only.
- [ ] Shot 8 gets **no LUT** — pure clean white. This contrast is intentional and impactful.
- [ ] Transitions: **straight cuts** for Shots 1→2, 3→4 (propulsive). **Dissolve 12 frames** for Shots 4→5 (the emotional pivot). **Long dissolve 20 frames** for 6→7 and 7→8 (contemplative close).
- [ ] BGM: fingerpicked acoustic guitar intro → builds to warm piano + soft beat → peaks at Shot 5 → strips back to near-silence at Shot 6 → resolves on Shot 7 → single chord on Shot 8. Target ~90 BPM. Reference: Novo Amor, Boy Pablo, Mac DeMarco acoustic.
- [ ] Caption style: **thin white serif** or light-weight sans, centered, at 35% from bottom. Subtle shadow only. No animation — just a clean fade in at 0.4s. Hold through full shot. Do NOT use bold kinetic captions — this video's tone is quiet.
- [ ] Sound design: airplane hum fade (S1) · soft bell ambient (S2) · sizzle + chatter (S3) · dead silence with one tap (S4) · crowd energy + laughter (S5) · distant city (S6) · notification chime (S7) · silence (S8).
- [ ] Export: **1080×1920 · 9:16 · 24fps** (not 30fps — 24fps is what makes this feel cinematic, not app-demo-like).

## Why This Works

**The app doesn't appear until 76% of the way through.** This is intentional and counterintuitive — and it's why this video works for *brand* while the feature videos work for *activation*. By the time KeepTrip's name appears, the viewer has already felt what it's like to be fully in a trip. The notification in Shot 7 isn't an ad — it's a relief. The brand earns its appearance.

**The one "bad" shot (S4)** is the most important creative decision. Showing the stress — the empty bowls, the calculator, the friends waiting — creates real tension in 4 seconds flat. Without it, Shots 5 and 6 are just pretty travel footage. With it, they become *relief*. Contrast is what generates emotion.

**Shot 6 is the emotional climax, not the CTA.** Most brand videos build to a logo reveal. This one builds to a quiet moment on a bridge — a woman looking at a river. That's where the feeling lands. The brand reveal comes *after* the emotion, not as the emotion. This order matters.

**24fps and the consistent trio** make this feel real. The same three people in the same clothes across 8 shots is what makes the viewer invest in them as people, not actors. AI video generators need this kind of consistency reinforcement in every prompt.

**The caption in Shot 6** — *"The moment you stayed in — instead of scrolling through"* — is the emotional core of the whole video. It names the exact behavior KeepTrip enables without ever mentioning the app. That specificity of insight is what makes a brand video feel intelligent rather than generic.

**This video is for awareness, not conversion.** It pairs with the Jastip and Split Bill feature videos in a content funnel: this runs at the top (cold audiences, discovery), those run at the middle (warm audiences who already follow or know the app). Different jobs, different tones.
