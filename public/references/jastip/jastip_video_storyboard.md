# TravTrack — "Jastip" Feature Launch Video

**Format:** Instagram Reel / TikTok · **Duration:** 30s · **Shots:** 6 (~5s each) · **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 (1080×1920)
**Style:** Modern & clean premium motion graphic · **Language:** English · **Structure:** Problem → Solution → Proof → CTA
**CTA:** Download / update the app

---

## Visual Theme (lock this across every shot)

| Layer | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Palette** | Brand gold `#DB9F5C` (accent/CTA) · ink charcoal `#1A1A1E` (text/dark end card) · warm off-white `#F7F5F2` (surfaces) · slate `#8A8F98` (secondary text) · success green `#3BA55D` (paid/checkmark) · coral `#E86A5C` (used **only** for the problem/stress beats) |
| **Render look** | Flat **2.5D vector** motion design — crisp UI mockups on soft solid backgrounds, 2% grain, gentle long soft-shadows. **No** heavy gradients, no skeuomorphism. Premium travel/fintech aesthetic. |
| **Lighting** | Soft motivated key from top-left, diffused ambient fill, delicate drop shadows for parallax depth. |
| **Depth / lens** | Near-orthographic UI with slight perspective tilt on the device frame; shallow blur on background layers; 2–3 parallax layers. |
| **Motion language** | Base = smooth ease-in-out `cubic-bezier(.4,0,.2,1)`. Hero moments get **spring/overshoot** (rolling numbers, checkmark, CTA). Staggered entrances (40–60ms offset). Locked frame with gentle scale "push-ins" and lateral parallax between shots. **Odometer**-style rolling money numbers. |
| **Type** | Geometric sans (Inter / SF Pro / Poppins). Bold kinetic headlines, medium for UI, **tabular figures** for money. |
| **Device / glyph** | One consistent iPhone frame; the real Jastip tab is the hero. **🎁** is the Jastip glyph/avatar. |

## Why This Works

- **The hook (S1)** opens on a universally relatable pain — your phone blowing up with "buy me this" — in the first second. That stops the scroll before anyone knows it's an ad.
- **Problem → Solution → Proof → CTA (Pattern B):** two problem beats (chaos, then money-math) build real tension, so the Shot 3 "snap to order" reveal lands as *relief*. That collapse-into-order is the emotional turn of the whole piece.
- **Pacing:** ~5s/shot matches average feed attention, and the solution arrives by the 10s mark — before the typical drop-off — with the satisfying payoffs stacked across 15–25s.
- **Money is the dopamine:** odometer numbers, the automatic "≈ home currency" conversion, the big "Total owed to you", and the checkmark celebration are inherently satisfying, screenshot-worthy beats — this is what makes a finance-y feature *feel* good.
- **Consistency = one intentional piece:** a single gold/off-white palette, one device frame, and one eased-spring motion language make six separate clips read as one product, not a mismatched montage.
- **Platform-native conversion:** vertical 9:16, captions-first for sound-off viewing, and a clean end card with download badges turn attention into installs.
